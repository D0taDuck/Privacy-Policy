# Tap Target Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 8, 2026**

Tap Target is committed to protecting the privacy and safety of its users.

## Information Collection

Tap Target does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

## Local Game Data

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on the user's device. This information is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

## Third-Party Services

Tap Target does not currently use advertising, analytics, account services, or other third-party services that collect personal information.

## Children's Privacy

Tap Target does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

## Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Tap Target's features or data practices change.

## Contact

Questions regarding this privacy policy may be submitted through the developer contact information provided on the Tap Target Google Play listing.
